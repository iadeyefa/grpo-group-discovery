Executive Summary & Evaluation Guide

Evaluation Goal & Prediction Mechanics: We measure how accurately discovered belief groups predict a country's stances on held-out (unseen) survey prompts. We hide 20% of questions during clustering, calculate each group's average stance on the remaining 80% training questions, and then test how accurately a group's average consensus predicts member countries' answers on the unseen test questions.

Prediction Lift (Higher = Better): Accuracy gain on held-out questions when using group consensus averages instead of a single global average baseline (jumping from 36.1% baseline to 68.3% accuracy = +0.3216 / +32.16 percentage points lift).
Mean JSD (Higher = Better): How different the opinion distributions are between clusters. Higher values mean groups genuinely disagree with each other, not just random noise.
Cohesion (Higher = Better): How much countries within the same cluster agree with each other (0 to 1 scale; 1.0 = identical preferences).
Davies-Bouldin (Lower = Better): How cleanly separated the clusters are from each other. Lower values mean tighter, non-overlapping groups with clear boundaries.
